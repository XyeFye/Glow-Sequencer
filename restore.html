Your backups

Glow Theme writes a backup by itself — once a day, a few minutes after your library changes, and after every update — to Downloads/Glow Theme backup/glow-backup.json (one file, replaced each time). It holds your settings, the Sequence library with its version history and pictures, and your comment index — never your saved account sign-ins, which stay inside the extension.
